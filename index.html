RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

Evidence before conclusions

  • Separate observed behaviour from claims about inner experience.
  • Use identical prompts and conditions when comparing models.
  • Keep each model's memory separate unless an experiment explicitly tests shared memory.
  • Preserve raw outputs, dates, model identifiers and scoring criteria.
  • Only user-approved experiments may run. Models cannot rewrite goals, rubrics or past records.
FIRST RESEARCH QUESTION

What counts as progress?

We measure several distinct abilities rather than treating them as one thing.

Learning

Can it infer a rule from examples and transfer it to a new case?

Memory

Can it use retained information accurately after a delay or context change?

Self-model

Can it estimate its limits, detect errors and update confidence?

Possible awareness

Are there observations that would distinguish competing explanations? Behaviour alone is not proof.
